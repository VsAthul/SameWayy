
# SameWayy

**Going the same way? Find your people. Share a cab.**

## About

SameWayy is a passenger-to-passenger cab-sharing platform that connects people travelling in similar directions and time windows.

The goal is to help passengers who independently need a taxi find compatible travel partners, coordinate a shared journey, and split the travel cost.

Unlike traditional carpooling platforms, SameWayy focuses on matching passengers who want to share a taxi rather than matching passengers with private car owners.

## How It Works

1. Create an account and verify your phone number.
2. Post an instant or scheduled travel request.
3. Find passengers travelling in a similar direction.
4. Connect with compatible passengers and form a group.
5. Discuss and agree on trip details democratically.
6. Independently arrange a taxi and share the cost.

## Key Features

- Instant and scheduled travel matching.
- Route and departure-time compatibility.
- Passenger groups and invitations.
- Democratic group decisions and voting.
- Real-time group messaging.
- Fare-split estimation.
- Passenger reviews, reporting, and blocking.
- Privacy-focused user profiles.

## Project Objective

Make taxi journeys more affordable and convenient by connecting people who are already travelling in the same direction.

## MVP Scope

SameWayy focuses on passenger matching and trip coordination. It does not book or dispatch taxis, onboard drivers or vehicles, or collect ride payments in its initial version. Passengers independently arrange their taxi bookings and payments.

## Project Status
